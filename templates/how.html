How FormCheck AI works

Understand how your documents are processed and checked.

01

Upload

Choose one document for field validation or two documents for cross-document comparison. Supported formats are PDF, PNG, JPG and JPEG.

02

Extract

PyMuPDF extracts selectable PDF text. Tesseract OCR reads scanned PDF pages and image uploads when needed.

03

Validate

FormCheck AI checks required fields and basic formats for name, date of birth, email and phone number.

04

Compare & review

When two documents contain the same detected field, their values are compared and differences are highlighted in the report.

Important

This is an assistance tool, not an official identity-verification service. Always review extracted details and original documents manually before submission.
