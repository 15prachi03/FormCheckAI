Help & support

Quick answers to common questions about document analysis.

Which file formats are supported?

PDF, PNG, JPG and JPEG files are supported. You can upload up to two documents per analysis.

Why could a field not be detected?

The field may be absent, printed in an unexpected format, or difficult for OCR to read. Try a clearer scan with good lighting and the full page visible.

Are my original files saved?

No. The original uploaded files are removed after processing. The extracted values and report summary are saved locally in SQLite to support history and reports.

Does a mismatch prove that a document is fraudulent?

No. A mismatch is a signal that values differ and should be reviewed. OCR mistakes and legitimate updates can also cause differences.

What if Tesseract OCR is not installed?

Install Tesseract OCR on your computer and ensure its executable is available in your system PATH. The Python package alone does not install the OCR engine.
